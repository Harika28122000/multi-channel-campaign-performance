Attribution & media mix

Multi-touch attribution modeling

When ten channels touch one purchase, which ones actually deserve the credit?

PythonMarkov chainsShapley valueMedia mix
23%Budget reallocation modeled
9-dayTracking gap corrected
2Models cross-validated

Started from an existing last-click model crediting nearly 40% of conversions to branded paid search — a channel that tends to close sales other touchpoints already generated. Rebuilt attribution with a Markov chain removal-effect model, cross-checked against Shapley value contributions in Python, and worked through a nine-day gap in UTM tracking from a tagging outage by imputing traffic from historical channel mix instead of dropping the period. The corrected model shifted credit toward upper-funnel social and email, alongside a clear note on what the model still can't see — offline word-of-mouth, iOS tracking loss.

Lifecycle marketing

Churn prediction & lifecycle segmentation

Win-back campaigns were converting at 2% — which customers were we even talking to?

PythonClusteringChurn modelLifecycle marketing
5Lifecycle segments
1Model, tracked precision
MDE-setTest plan

Traced an underperforming win-back campaign back to its targeting logic, which treated every lapsed subscriber as one group. Built a churn prediction model in Python, then clustered predicted churners by likely cause rather than risk score alone — a customer who left over price and one who left after a support issue need different messaging entirely. Mapped each segment to a specific offer and email variant, then designed an A/B test with a calculated minimum detectable effect so the eventual read would hold up.
